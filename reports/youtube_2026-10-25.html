AI-driven collapse of hacking costs 5
LLM agents became markedly better at offensive and defensive cyber starting around January, and the cost of running them across many targets has since fallen about 15x on the guest's internal efficiency measure, with a further 10x drop expected every 6-12 months.

So what: Cyber risk is now an economics problem: attack cost trending to zero implies attack volume trending far higher; favours security vendors and agentic defense spend.
Hacking has shifted from a bespoke, human-expensive skill (weeks of expert time) to a scalable 'factory' that can hit a website for about $10 with no humans, so attackers no longer need to justify ROI per target.

Monetization of mass breaches 5
Threat actors are monetizing cheap mass hacking of previously unprofitable targets (e.g. dentists, healthcare) via extortion, threatening to breach or leak unless paid; this is described as a now-scalable cybercrime motion.
causal🗣 Hari, CEO of Canina Securityconviction: highhealthcareextortionthreat actors
So what: Expect rising ransom/extortion incidents beyond crypto and finance; relevant for cyber insurance loss ratios and healthcare IT security spend.
Crypto was historically the prime target (North Korea funded its missile program via crypto hacks) because payouts were high and on-chain; with attack costs near zero, attacker attention is turning from crypto toward broad Web2.
anecdote🗣 Hari, CEO of Canina Securityconviction: mediumNorth KoreacryptoWeb2
So what: Crypto's relative share of attacks may fall even as absolute attack counts rise.
Leaked personal databases enable second- and third-order attacks, such as the Ledger leak from 2021 linked to physical 'wrench attacks' on identified high-net-worth holders, so harm is hard to quantify from the breach alone.
causal🗣 Odysius, CEO of Filixconviction: mediumLedgerwrench attacks
So what: Crypto holders whose identity is tied to leaked data face physical-security risk; privacy/KYC data minimisation matters.
AI agents let attackers combine leaked data and personal context to craft individualized, believable scams at millions-of-people scale, e.g. industrialized elder scams; one engineer's chatbot recited his compensation and partners, showing how much personal data AI products now hold.
causal🗣 Hari, CEO of Canina Securityconviction: mediumChatGPTelder scamspersonal data

Offense is expected to dominate for the next 12-18 months, and the recommended defense is to attack yourself with an agentic offensive tool and fix every finding, aiming to raise attacker cost from about $1 to about $10,000, which deters most opportunistic attackers.

Practical guidance for crypto holders 3
Individuals should audit which products hold their data and money, ask providers what they are doing about security, and move assets from any provider whose security program has not changed.

So what: Security posture becomes a competitive differentiator for exchanges, wallets and apps; disclosures could drive flows.
Assume any service will be breached and design for redundancy and limited blast radius, e.g. self-custody with multiple independent devices so several supply chains must fail at once; protocols are also buying orthogonal second-code-base protection.
