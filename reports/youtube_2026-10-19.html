Fin-Syn ownership economics 7
Under the FCC financial interest and syndication rules (Fin-Syn, 1970 to 1993), broadcast networks could not own the shows they aired. They paid studios roughly 60 to 80 percent of production cost for a limited license (deficit financing), and the studio kept the IP and all downstream syndication, international and home video rights.
